# Governed 3D Anatomy Module

The 3D system remains private and fail-closed. Eight regional review slots cover cervical, shoulder, elbow, wrist/hand, lumbar, hip, knee, and ankle/foot. The schema supports bones, joints, articular surfaces, ligaments, tendons, muscles, origins/insertions, nerves, relevant vessels, landmarks, planes, axes, and regional relationships.

The interaction contract includes rotate, zoom, pan, reset, isolate, hide/show, transparency, selection, labels, origin/insertion, muscle action, related content, and keyboard controls. Every scene requires a structure browser, relationship text, keyboard equivalence, transcript, WebGL fallback, reduced-motion behaviour, lazy loading, and reviewed mobile polygon/texture/decoded-memory budgets.

No governed model asset or reviewed anatomy label is present. All eight regional slots remain unassigned and ineligible for public use. The public route count, public model asset count, and unrelated-route 3D bundle bytes are all zero.

The private source-candidate ledger now records three external archive/package records without copying their binaries into Git. The exact pinned `Z-Biomechanics.7z` was also independently verified by byte size, SHA-256 and Git blob identity. The upstream source licence is CC BY-SA 4.0 and requires the stated Z-Anatomy and BodyParts3D attribution plus share-alike handling. That verifies the licence family only. It does not approve a selected derivative, its structure mapping, its attribution package, or public use.

The companion local biomechanics archive contains five technically identifiable GLB files and 18 proposed movement definitions. Its clinical ranges, muscle roles, conditions, tests, and movement behaviour remain unreviewed.
