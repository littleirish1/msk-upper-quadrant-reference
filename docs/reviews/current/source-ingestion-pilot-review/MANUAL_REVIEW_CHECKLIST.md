- [ ] Sample quarantined records without copying matched values into review notes.

## Extraction and references

- [ ] Compare a sample of PDF page and presentation slide provenance against the private originals.
- [ ] Review extraction warnings and unsupported legacy formats.
- [ ] Check a sample of citation candidates for over- and under-extraction.
- [ ] Verify DOI, PMID and URL candidates externally in a later approved phase.
- [ ] Review exact and probable duplicate groups.

## Clinical proposals

- [ ] Review RCRSP topic mapping and evidence questions.

- [ ] Review the ankle leaflet classification and verification queue.
- [ ] Confirm no proposed wording is treated as an approved clinical claim.
- [ ] Record clinician decisions explicitly; do not infer approval.

## Boundaries

- [ ] Inspect `out` for zero AI-manager, private-cache, source-intake, GLB, or 3D-route output.
- [ ] Confirm public cases and diagnosis reveal are unchanged.
