# Privacy, Licensing And Publication Controls

This is an engineering and governance contract, not legal approval.

- Personal and health information must not enter public content, packets, logs,
  analytics, or learner state.
- Teaching-source intake remains private and requires explicit clearance for
  derivative use.
- Copyright, ownership, licence, attribution, modification history, clinical
  review, and accessibility review are independent gates for visual assets.
- Analytics, cookies, accounts, forms, and learner-data collection are disabled.
- The intended audience remains physiotherapy and allied-health learners and
  practitioners; the educational disclaimer does not replace professional
  judgement or local policy.
- Takedown or safety concerns require content withdrawal, preservation of the
  audit trail, owner notification, triage, and reviewed correction.
- Retention, legal basis, incident handling, and publication approval require
  human information-governance and legal decisions.
