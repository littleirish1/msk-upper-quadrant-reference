# Anatomy Visual Asset Policy

## Public Eligibility

- Use only assets with recorded source, author, licence, attribution requirements, and modification history.
- Do not infer or invent provenance.
- GLB and other 3D assets remain private until asset, performance, accessibility, and governance review is complete.
- External assets are not imported during ordinary content scaffolding.

## Preferred Workflow

- Prefer accessible HTML, CSS, and reviewed SVG diagrams for simple teaching visuals.
- Record a stable asset ID, source, licence, checksum, creation or modification history, linked content IDs, and review state.
- Preserve original attribution and licence notices where required.
- Provide useful alternative text and a text-equivalent explanation.
- Keep draft assets outside public directories and static output.

## Review

Visuals require technical review for rendering and accessibility, provenance/licence review, and clinician review for anatomical and clinical accuracy. No visual should imply diagnostic certainty or replace examination guidance.
