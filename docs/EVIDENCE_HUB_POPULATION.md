# Private Evidence Hub Population

The Evidence Hub now contains three private metadata-only evidence records generated from genuine tracked sources in the ingestion register. Each record pins the exact repository locator and SHA-256 checksum, states that bibliographic identity and clinical claims are unverified, and remains draft/unreviewed/public-ineligible.

No title is presented as a paper title, and no author, DOI, PMID, study finding, recommendation, appraisal, clearance, relationship, supersession, or approval has been invented. The public projection remains empty.

The private graph report reconciles module revisions, Patient Truth hashes, movement records, 3D slots, and MCQ authoring slots to explicit gap collections. Conditions, clinical questions, guidelines, reviews, primary studies, claims, presentation variants, approved anatomy/movement evidence, and review decisions remain zero because the available repository material does not support honest population of those categories.
