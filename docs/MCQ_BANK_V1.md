# Governed MCQ Bank

The Version 1 MCQ bank contains all 20 planned stable authoring slots with region, target content, learning objective, difficulty state, evidence/reference relationships, after-submission reveal policy, competence-claim prohibition, review states, and explicit blockers.

No clinical stems, distractors, answers, or explanations are authored because the required source/evidence relationships and human reviews do not exist. All 20 records therefore remain `source-insufficient`, private, and absent from Search/static output.

The schema strictly requires at least three options, unique option IDs, exactly one best answer, and an explanation for every option whenever content is later authored. Public eligibility additionally requires approved lifecycle, evidence, and clinical/evidence/source/accessibility/publication review for the exact revision. A separate synthetic governance example tests the contract without pretending to be clinical content.
