# Version 1 Regional Content Production Matrix

The governed matrix reconciles cervical, shoulder, elbow, wrist/hand, thoracic, headache, lumbar, hip, knee, ankle/foot, and cross-cutting neuro/systemic differentials across 19 required domains: anatomy, landmarks, muscle roles, conditions, presentations, subjective/objective assessment, neurological screening, tests, differentials, red flags, imaging/investigations, management, prognosis, evidence, cases, MCQs, movement, and 3D anatomy.

Existing live-region content is recorded as preserved baseline structure; this matrix does not reopen or extend its clinical approval. Unsupported cells are explicit production gaps. Planned and cross-cutting areas remain non-routable. Exact evidence/module relationships, movement/3D links, source clearance, and human review remain required for new work.

The matrix contains 11 areas × 19 domains (209 cells), creates zero clinical claims, and adds zero public routes.
