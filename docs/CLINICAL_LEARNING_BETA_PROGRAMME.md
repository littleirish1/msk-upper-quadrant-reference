# Clinical learning beta programme

## Status

The programme is planned but has not started. The repository records no real participants, sessions, feedback, outcomes, consent decisions, or publication approval.

## Cohorts and tasks

Four planned cohorts—physiotherapy students, Band 5 clinicians, experienced MSK clinicians, and clinical educators—each receive the same four route-based tasks. The tasks examine navigation, mode comprehension, diagnosis disclosure, anatomy governance, search, keyboard flow, and mobile wayfinding without soliciting clinical or health information.

## Privacy and consent

Sessions must use opaque identifiers. Collection is limited to structured task feedback and must exclude patient, participant health, and unnecessary identifying data. Consent wording, privacy notice, retention period, recruitment, incentives, and publication require accountable human approval before use.

## Feedback and escalation

Feedback categories are navigation, content clarity, diagnosis disclosure, accessibility, performance, privacy, and clinical concern. Severity runs from observation through blocker. Blockers stop the session; clinical and privacy concerns enter their accountable review queues.

## Synthetic fixtures

The four generated records are labelled `fixture: true`, use `synthetic-session-*` identifiers, and exist only to test ingestion and triage. They are excluded from counts and must never be represented as research or beta results.

## Dashboard boundary

Beta tasks appear only in the loopback private authoring workspace. No public beta dashboard, submission endpoint, analytics collector, or persistence API is added.
