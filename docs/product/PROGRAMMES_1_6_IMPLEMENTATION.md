## Representative Or Scaffolded

workspace, surveillance adapter, beta model, and operational adapters are
representative governed foundations. They are not complete clinical libraries,
production services, or evidence approvals.

## Outstanding Human Gates

privacy/legal review, accessibility sign-off, beta execution, independent
review, publication, merge, push, and deployment remain human-controlled.
