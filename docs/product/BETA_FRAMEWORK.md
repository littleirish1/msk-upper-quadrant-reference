# Governed Beta Framework

No beta participants, sessions, consent decisions, or results are represented
by this repository.

Planned groups are physiotherapy students, Band 5 clinicians, experienced MSK
clinicians, and clinical educators. Human-reviewed scripts should cover task
completion, content clarity, diagnosis disclosure, navigation, accessibility,
performance, privacy, and clinical concerns.

Feedback uses opaque session IDs and excludes health data. Consent wording,
privacy information, recruitment, retention, incentives, and publication of
findings require human governance review. Findings are triaged as observation,
low, medium, high, or blocker; clinical and privacy concerns escalate outside
automated publication.
