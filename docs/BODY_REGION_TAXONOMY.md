## Principles

- Do not expose planned modules in public navigation until reviewed content exists.
- Use slugs consistently across source metadata, templates, imports, cases, presentations, and evidence notes.
- Prefer body-region tags over one hardcoded region when a source spans multiple areas.
- Keep upper-quadrant naming as Phase 1 history, not the long-term platform boundary.
