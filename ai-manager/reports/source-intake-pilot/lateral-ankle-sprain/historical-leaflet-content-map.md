# Historical ankle leaflet content map

## Classification

The January 2019 local patient-information leaflet is a historical/local-practice source. It requires current evidence cross-check and is not authoritative by itself.

## Topic map

Candidate topics: injury explanation, symptoms, early management, loading advice, exercises, recovery expectations, return-to-sport progression, and escalation advice.

## Interpretation
