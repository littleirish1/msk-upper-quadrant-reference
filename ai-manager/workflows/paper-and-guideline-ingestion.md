# Paper And Guideline Workflow

1. Intake approved source and manifest.
2. Hash exact source bytes.
3. Capture bibliographic and rights metadata.
4. Extract text without changing the source.
5. Classify evidence type and limitations.
6. Extract each proposed claim with a precise source location.
7. Compare claims with existing content IDs.
8. Draft a proposed update outside public content.
9. Verify every citation and identifier independently.
10. Obtain human clinical review.
11. Generate a focused Git diff.
12. Record approval or rejection.
13. Commit through the normal reviewed workflow.
14. Archive according to approved retention rules.

Automated tools may propose but must not silently replace recommendations, prognosis, diagnostic values, or management guidance.
