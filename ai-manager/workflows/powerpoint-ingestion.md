# PowerPoint Ingestion

## Intake

- Record a source ID, checksum, rights status, intended use, and identifiable-material check.
- Keep the original deck private; do not copy it into public content or tracked source folders.

## Extraction Report

For each slide, record slide number, visible text, speaker-note text, cited sources, and whether a statement appears to be lecturer opinion or source-supported. Do not infer missing citations.

## Review

- Flag patient/staff-identifiable material and stop normal processing.
- Map useful material to stable content IDs.
- Propose cases, questions, or updates as private drafts.
- Preserve slide-level provenance.
- Require citation verification and clinician review before use.

The workflow does not implement OCR or ingest a real deck in this phase.
