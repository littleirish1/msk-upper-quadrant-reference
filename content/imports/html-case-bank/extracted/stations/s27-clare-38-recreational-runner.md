## Scenario history

**Clare, 38, recreational runner.** Six-month unilateral headache starting in the upper neck and radiating to the temple. Triggered by sustained desk posture and long drives. Pain 3–5/10, settles with movement. Restricted and painful upper cervical rotation to the symptomatic side. Headaches reproduced with sustained upper cervical extension. Wants to keep training for a 10K in six weeks.

## Scenario examination / objective findings

Cervical AROM: restricted and mildly painful in upper cervical rotation to symptomatic side. FRT: restricted rotation toward symptomatic side with reproduction of familiar headache. CCFT: performs to 24mmHg only (population mean 26-28mmHg). Upper cervical palpation: C1-C2 right hypomobility and local tenderness concordant with headache. Neurological exam: normal. Cranial nerve screen: normal. BP: normal.

## Safety flags / red flags

SNOOPP screen: all negative. No thunderclap onset. No neurological deficit. No systemic features. No new/changing headache pattern. BP normal.

## Legacy diagnosis

Cervicogenic Headache (CGH) from C1-C2 dysfunction

## Legacy reasoning

Unilateral headache from upper neck radiating to temple + provoked by sustained cervical loading + positive FRT (restricted rotation AND headache reproduction toward ipsilateral side) + C1-C2 hypomobility on palpation = CGH from C1-C2 dysfunction. Pain settles with movement (opposite of migraine). No autonomic features. SNOOPP negative. FRT: Ogince 2007 — good sensitivity/specificity for CGH within diagnostic cluster. CCFT: DNF dysfunction consistent with neck pain population.

## Clinical traps

Calling this migraine because of the temple/periorbital distribution without completing the FRT. The FRT is the discriminating test — restriction AND symptom reproduction toward the ipsilateral side points to C1-C2. A good answer says CGH; an outstanding answer explains the C1-C2 mechanism via the trigemino-cervical nucleus and uses the FRT findings to justify. Also: not addressing the running goal — the outstanding answer integrates the patient's goal into the management plan from the outset.

## Legacy domain content

### 1. Most Likely Diagnosis

- Icon: 🔵
- Badge: db-diag

**Cervicogenic Headache (CGH).**

Three features: (1) unilateral headache originating from upper neck radiating to temple = CGH anatomical distribution via trigemino-cervical nucleus; (2) triggered by sustained desk posture and long drives = cervical loading provocation; (3) restricted and painful upper cervical rotation toward symptomatic side + headache reproduced with sustained upper cervical extension = positive CGH examination findings.

### 2. Key Differentials + Discriminator

- Icon: 🔵
- Badge: db-diag

- **Migraine:** Episodic, moderate-severe, photophobia/phonophobia/nausea/aura. _Against here:_ no autonomic features, settles with movement, clear cervical provocation. _Important:_ cervical MSK can contribute TO migraine — the 4-quadrant model applies (Thomson/McElhill).
- **TTH:** Bilateral, pressing/tightening, not worsened by cervical movement. _Against here:_ unilateral, specific cervical provocation, upper neck origin.

### 3. SNOOPP Safety Screen

- Icon: 🔴
- Badge: db-safety

**SNOOPP — All Negative Here**

Systemic signs: none. Neurologic symptoms: none. Onset: gradual, not thunderclap. Older: 38yo. Progressive: stable. Positional change/papilloedema: none.

Document that SNOOPP screen was completed and negative. BP check appropriate. Appropriate to proceed with cervical assessment.

### 4. Two Tests + Interpretive Value

- Icon: 🟡
- Badge: db-obj

- **FRT (Flexion Rotation Test):** Full cervical flexion + rotation both sides. _Positive = restriction AND symptom reproduction toward ipsilateral side = C1-C2 dysfunction driving headache via trigemino-cervical nucleus. Normal 44° each side; C1-C2 dysfunction ~17° less rotation (Hall 2008, Ogince 2007)._
- **CCFT (Cranio-Cervical Flexion Test):** Pressure biofeedback, 20-30mmHg in 2mmHg increments. _Positive = unable to maintain target pressure = deep neck flexor dysfunction. Population mean 26-28mmHg, neck pain mean 22-24mmHg._

### 5. Management + Return to 10K

- Icon: 🟡
- Badge: db-mgmt

- Education: cervical contribution to headache; running is NOT contraindicated
- Upper cervical manual therapy (C1-C2 mobilisation/SNAG) as adjunct to exercise
- DNF endurance exercise (CCFT baseline as starting point)
- Cervical rotation mobility exercise; posture correction
- Running advice: head position, surface, pacing — symptom response guides return
- SEEDS: Sleep, Exercise, Eat, Diary, Stress
