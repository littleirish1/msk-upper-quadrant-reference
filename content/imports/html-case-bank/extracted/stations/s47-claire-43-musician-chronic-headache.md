## Scenario history

**Claire, 43, musician (pianist).** 11-year history of migraine (21 headache days/month, VAS 6/10). Also has constant neck pain and stiffness (VAS 3-4/10), worse with sustained piano practice. Failed multiple migraine preventers. On Botox for chronic migraine. Neck pain worsens during migraine postdrome. Wants to understand whether physiotherapy can help.

## Scenario examination / objective findings

FHP, restricted upper cervical rotation toward right (symptomatic side). FRT: restricted right rotation — familiar neck/head pain reproduced. TOP C2-C7 paraspinals bilaterally, bilateral MFT. Cranial nerve exam: normal. Neurological exam: normal. BP: 126/78.

## Safety flags / red flags

SNOOPP screen: all negative for secondary headache. Complex primary headache already under neurology (Botox). Cervical MSK disorder co-exists. IFOMPT screen: essential before any cervical intervention.

## Legacy diagnosis

Cervical MSK Disorder Contributing to Migraine — 4-Quadrant Model: Quadrant 4

## Legacy reasoning

Claire sits in 4-quadrant model quadrant 4 (Thomson/McElhill): migraine PLUS cervical MSK disorder that CONTRIBUTES to migraine frequency/severity. Not pure CGH. Not pure migraine. FRT restricted with symptom reproduction = C1-C2 dysfunction. Outstanding answer explains that treating the cervical component alone will not resolve migraine — co-management with neurology is essential.

## Clinical traps

Treating this as pure CGH or pure migraine. Also: not recognising that manual therapy can trigger migraine attacks in sensitised patients (Carvalho 2021) — if cervical treatment triggers a prolonged flare with photophobia/nausea, the migraine component is being provoked. Migraine with aura = relative contraindication to cervical manipulation. Medication overuse headache? (Always screen — BASH 2019: triptans/opioids >10 days/month).

## Legacy domain content

### 1. Clinical Picture

- Icon: 🔵
- Badge: db-diag

**Cervical MSK disorder contributing to migraine — the most complex headache presentation.**

The 4-quadrant model (Thomson/McElhill): this patient sits in quadrant 4 — migraine PLUS cervical MSK disorder that contributes to migraine frequency/severity. Not pure CGH. Not pure migraine. The distinction matters because: (1) treating the cervical component alone will not resolve migraine; (2) manual therapy can potentially trigger migraine attacks in sensitised patients (Carvalho 2021).

### 2. Distinguishing the Headache Types

- Icon: 🔵
- Badge: db-diag

- **Migraine component:** Episodic, moderate-severe, photophobia/phonophobia, nausea, 4-72hrs, +/- aura
- **Cervical MSK component:** Neck-originated unilateral headache, provoked by sustained postures, FRT restricted, cervical palpation reproduces headache
- **The combination:** Migraine frequency/severity worsened by cervical triggers; cervical pain co-exists with migraine; cervical stiffness worsens in the postdrome phase
- **Medication overuse:** BASH 2019 — always screen. Triptans/opioids >10 days/month → medication overuse headache

### 3. When Physiotherapy Can Trigger Migraine

- Icon: 🔴
- Badge: db-safety

**Treatment-Triggered Migraine — Clinical Trap**

Carvalho et al. (2021): neck muscle endurance exercises and manual therapy can trigger migraine attacks in sensitised patients. If treatment triggers a prolonged headache flare with migraine features (photophobia, nausea, phonophobia) — this signals the migraine component. Adapt approach.

IFOMPT screen essential before any cervical intervention. Migraine with aura = relative contraindication to cervical manipulation. Liaison with neurologist/GP for migraine prevention medication may be more appropriate than physiotherapy as primary treatment.

### 4. Assessment

- Icon: 🟡
- Badge: db-obj

- FRT — assess C1-C2 dysfunction component
- CCFT — DNF endurance

- SNOOPP screen: always complete

### 5. Management — Integrated Approach

- Icon: 🟡
- Badge: db-mgmt

- Co-management with GP/neurologist for migraine prevention medication
- Cervical physiotherapy: gentle, symptom-monitored; avoid triggering migraine attacks
- SEEDS framework: Sleep, Exercise, Eat, Diary, Stress — all contribute to migraine frequency
- Education: explain the 4-quadrant model; set realistic expectations — physiotherapy manages the cervical component, not the migraine itself
- Vestibular rehab if balance/dizziness component
- Medication overuse counselling if indicated
