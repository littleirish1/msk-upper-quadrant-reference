# Paper Imports

This folder is reserved for future paper and evidence-source imports.

## What Belongs Here

- Raw paper files, citation packages, or metadata files placed under `raw/`.
- Extracted citation metadata, abstracts, notes, or reviewer summaries placed under `extracted/`.
- Source records that can later link to evidence notes, cases, presentations, and condition pages.

## What Should Not Be Committed

- Copyrighted files that cannot be stored in the repository.
- Private or patient-identifiable material.
- Credentials, API keys, or local machine paths.
- Unreviewed summaries presented as final evidence.

## Review Status

Imported paper material is draft and unreviewed until checked. Evidence summaries and downstream teaching material should stay `needs-review` until a human reviewer approves them.

The long-term scope is whole-body physiotherapy clinical reasoning, not only upper quadrant content.
