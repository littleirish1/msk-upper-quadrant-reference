# Templates

This folder is reserved for reusable templates for future cases, presentations, and evidence notes.

## What Belongs Here

- Case templates under `cases/`.
- Presentation templates under `presentations/`.
- Evidence note templates under `evidence/`.
- Review checklist structures and draft output patterns.

## What Should Not Be Committed

- Private or patient-identifiable material.
- Credentials, API keys, or local machine paths.
- Generated clinical content presented as reviewed.

## Review Status

Templates should help generate draft material only. Any generated case, presentation, or evidence note is draft and unreviewed until checked by a human reviewer.

The long-term scope is whole-body physiotherapy clinical reasoning, not only upper quadrant content.
